## 🎯 Key Findings from Industry Research

### Existing Products Analysis

| **Product** | **What It Solves** | **What It Doesn't Solve** | **Your Opportunity** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SolarFlareNet** | Flare prediction (24-72h) | CME/SEP, infrastructure impact, business translation | Multi-event correlation + business intelligence |
| **Solar Activity AI** | Multi-modal data, autonomous forecasting | Commercial deployment, accessibility, enterprise integration | Commercial platform with enterprise features |
| **NOAA/SWPC** | Government alerts, public service | AI/ML integration, business metrics, automation | AI-powered automation + business translation |

1. ❌ **Data Standardization Crisis** - 40-70% of data incompatible
2. ❌ **Multi-Event Correlation** - No cascading event prediction
3. ❌ **Business Translation** - Scientific language, not business metrics
4. ❌ **Extended Forecasting** - Max 72 hours, need 7-14 days
5. ❌ **Physics-AI Integration** - Black box models, no physical constraints
6. ❌ **Economic Impact** - No $ loss quantification
7. ❌ **Enterprise Integration** - No API/integration capabilities
