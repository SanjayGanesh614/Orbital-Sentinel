# Quick Feature Summary - Space Weather AI

## 🚨 Top 5 Critical Missing Features

### 1. **Multi-Hour Forecasting** ⭐⭐⭐
- **What**: Predict storms 6-24 hours ahead
- **Why**: Operators need advance warning to take action
- **Impact**: Transforms from "current status" to "actionable forecast"
- **Effort**: 2-3 weeks

### 2. **Alert & Notification System** ⭐⭐⭐
- **What**: Email/SMS/Slack alerts when thresholds exceeded
- **Why**: Users can't monitor dashboard 24/7
- **Impact**: Enables proactive response
- **Effort**: 1-2 weeks

### 3. **Historical Database** ⭐⭐
- **What**: Store predictions, outcomes, track model performance
- **Why**: Can't improve without tracking accuracy
- **Impact**: Enables model improvement and compliance
- **Effort**: 2 weeks

### 4. **Regional Risk Mapping** ⭐⭐⭐
- **What**: Geographic visualization of risks (latitude-based)
- **Why**: Power grid risks vary by location
- **Impact**: Critical for infrastructure operators
- **Effort**: 2-3 weeks

### 5. **User Authentication** ⭐⭐
- **What**: Multi-user, multi-organization support
- **Why**: Required for enterprise adoption
- **Impact**: Enables SaaS business model
- **Effort**: 3-4 weeks
